Privacy

Updated November 22, 2026. Covers taylor-riley.com only.

What I collect

This site counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It records the page you opened, the site that linked you here, your browser and device type, your country, and how long the page took to load. I see these as totals, not as a profile of you.

Cloudflare also hosts the site, so it processes the normal details of each request, such as your IP address and browser, to serve the page and block abuse.

The pages work the same if you block the analytics script.

What I don't do

  • No advertising, ad networks, or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of visitor data.
  • No forms or accounts. If you email me, I use your message only to reply.

Other services

The older blog, game, and error pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives a request from your browser when you open them. The game keeps your best score in your browser's local storage; it never leaves your device. Links to GitHub and LinkedIn follow those sites' own policies.
